# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in Embertop to be a calm experience, in keeping with the
tool itself. Everyone is welcome regardless of experience, background, or
identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct and kind. Review the change, not the person.
- Assume the other person is working with different constraints than you.
- Accept that "no" is a reasonable answer for a project that is deliberately
  small in scope.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Posting real access logs, IP addresses, or credentials belonging to a system
  you do not own.

## Reporting

Report problems through the contact channel at
[limsumin.com](https://limsumin.com). Reports are handled privately.

The maintainer may edit, hide, or remove contributions that do not follow this
document, and may block accounts for repeated or severe behaviour.
